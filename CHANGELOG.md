# Changelog

Notable changes to Breath, newest first.

## v1.0.0 - August 14, 2026

Initial public release.

- Four practice spaces (Calm, Intimacy, Energy, Holotropic), each with multiple
  presets, each with its own honesty note shown in the app.
- One breathing orb with full-screen procedural sacred-geometry visualizers, all
  driven by a single shared breath signal.
- Synthesized audio: breath-tracking wash, hold-hush, optional voiced exhale
  (hum / Om) for Calm and Intimacy, ambient beds (Ocean, Rain, Deep Drone),
  warm fixed-pitch hold markers, and a completion chime.
- Guided descent pace mode for Calm, capped at the studied resonance pace for
  Coherent.
- Clean session endings planned to whole breath cycles, with a short landing rest.
- Together Mode: a shared five-in / seven-out rhythm for two people and one
  screen, with a twin-orb visual.
- Accurate round-based Tummo (Kozhevnikov et al. 2013) and a safety-gated
  Holotropic space with a session-length cap.
- Installable, offline-capable PWA (service worker, manifest, icons). Preferences
  stored in localStorage. No accounts, no analytics, no network requests.
- iOS screen-lock audio with lock-screen controls and interruption recovery.
  Android haptic cues (iOS Safari has no vibration API).
- Visual-quality settings with device-profiling auto-downgrade for older phones.
